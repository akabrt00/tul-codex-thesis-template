# Human review checklist

Select only checks relevant to the changed scope. Technical success never answers these questions automatically.

## Meaning and argument

- Does the text say what the author intends?
- Does each conclusion follow from the shown evidence?
- Is uncertainty, limitation, failure, or disagreement represented honestly?
- Does the section fit the aim and surrounding argument without repetition or contradiction?

## Facts and evidence

- Does every factual claim match the cited original page or project record?
- Are experiment IDs, samples, apparatus settings, values, units, and exclusions correct?
- Has the agent introduced a plausible-sounding but unsupported statement?

## Calculations and data

- Are inputs, units, assumptions, precision, uncertainty, and sign conventions appropriate?
- Does the selected model or statistical method make sense for the experiment?
- Are graphs and tables faithful to the underlying data?

## Visual and formal quality

- Is the figure necessary, readable, correctly captioned, and placed in the right context?
- Do title pages, names, programme data, page order, and official metadata match reality?
- Does the rendered PDF look coherent on the pages affected by the change?

## Review decision

- `approved`: the named scope and version are accepted by the user;
- `changes-requested`: corrections are required before approval;
- `needs-supervisor-review`: the author cannot responsibly decide alone.
