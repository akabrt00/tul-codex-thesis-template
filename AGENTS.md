- Read `AI_NA_TUL.md` before processing project inputs or producing assessed content. Use the provenance and article/page map for `SRC-0006`; the older `SRC-0005` must not override the newer supplied AI guidance. Do not claim formal repeal without evidence.
- Record substantive generative AI use in `POUZITI_AI.md`: tool, available version, affected deliverable and exact parts, purpose, agent checks and separate author verification. Do not invent versions or human verification. Ordinary spelling/typography is exempt unless the teacher required otherwise in advance.
- Use the registered document key at the start of each ledger part cell (`main: ...`); run `scripts/check-ai-evidence.ps1` at grouped checkpoints. Its WARN/FAIL report checks completeness and PDF declaration presence only, never policy compliance or human approval.
- Before submission, reconcile the evidence with a truthful declaration in the assessed output using the applicable institutional/publisher form. Do not silently alter official IS STAG pages. AI is not a coauthor; do not impose the older blanket ban on bibliography entries.
- The author must independently verify facts and sources and explain every autonomous step, intermediate result and reasoning. Synthetic AI data must be explicitly described, justified and labeled in the methodology; never pass it off as empirical measurement.
- BEFORE reading or sending protected project inputs to an AI system, establish that TUL approves that specific system/configuration. Directive article 3 covers unpublished scientific data, licensed or trade-secret code, sensitive data, nonpublic university documents and third-party personal data. User consent, a paid account, disabled training or local file storage are insufficient evidence of institutional approval. This applies to the main cloud agent as well as external AI plugins.
- If approval is unknown, do not inspect protected content in that AI environment; use public, synthetic or safely anonymized inputs or a workflow in an approved environment. Approval status and the data scope belong in `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`. Never disclose protected content merely to classify it.
- The supplied directive names Gemini as approved and DeepSeek as prohibited at its effective date. It does not establish Codex/OpenAI approval. Verify current TUL conditions and NUKIB warnings before choosing a system; prohibited systems cannot be used even for public inputs.
- Respect previously announced teacher restrictions. Direct knowledge assessment excludes AI unless explicitly allowed in the assignment; voice synthesis/cloning and student video avatars in the specified distance/audio-video assessments require prior explicit teacher consent. Distinguish defense preparation from AI assistance during an examination.
- Treat AI-detector results as neither direct nor sole proof of misconduct; authorship assessment belongs to authorized humans. Apply external publisher rules where relevant.
