# Review Thesis Chapter

## Review before rewriting

Resolve the exact chapter and its declared purpose. Read enough neighboring material to judge continuity, but keep the reported scope explicit. Unless the user directly asks for corrections, diagnose only and do not edit.

Check:

- section and paragraph logic, transitions, purpose, repetition, and unclear wording;
- claims without support, claims stronger than their evidence, and missing qualifications;
- terminology, abbreviations, figure and table references, units, and cross-references;
- whether results are interpreted rather than merely repeated;
- whether section conclusions follow from the shown methods, evidence, and data.

Use `$manage-project-sources` to verify citation-bearing claims against registered original pages. Never use model memory as an expert source. Use `$verify-scientific-calculations` for derived numerical claims and `$audit-figures-and-tables` when visual evidence is central.

## Report and optionally repair

List findings first, ordered by severity: `critical`, `major`, `minor`, or `suggestion`. For each, give location, problem, evidence, impact, and proposed action. Separate academic problems from stylistic preferences.

When corrections are explicitly requested, use `$tul-thesis`, preserve the author's intended meaning, recheck affected claims and references, compile as appropriate, and finish with `$review-agent-output` for the changed scope.
